ABOUT BENCH21

Built on decades of hands-on experience.

BENCH21 is the independent technical workshop of Andreas Kkallis, built on more than twenty years of practical work with electronics, electrical systems, computers and custom technology.

His work over the years has included electronic devices, antenna systems, computers, amplifiers, power electronics, remote-control systems, automation and purpose-built circuits.

BENCH21 continues with the same practical mindset today: understand the requirement, investigate the options and create a solution that makes sense.
